ℹ️ About & Notes

Brainbridge AI is an offline Class 10 Physics study tool designed to make wave concepts visual, interactive and easier to revise.

📌 Core Notes

Sound is mechanical; light and radio waves are electromagnetic. Refraction normally keeps frequency constant while speed and wavelength may change.

📖 Glossary

Amplitude — maximum displacement.
Wavelength — distance between consecutive same-phase points.
Frequency — cycles per second.
Time Period — time for one cycle.
Crest — highest transverse point.
Trough — lowest transverse point.
Compression — closely packed region in a longitudinal wave.
Rarefaction — spread-out region in a longitudinal wave.
Wavefront — equal-phase points joined together.
